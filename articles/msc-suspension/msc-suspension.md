## The problem

My MSc dissertation at the University of Leeds asked how far suspension design and active control could improve the dynamics and stability of an electric vehicle.

## What I did

- Engineered the suspension components in CAD and built multi-body dynamics models in MATLAB, Simulink and Simscape.
- Checked the structural integrity of the components with FEA in ANSYS.
- Ran virtual vehicle-dynamics tests: constant-radius cornering, stability-index analysis, and static and dynamic load cases.
- Applied DFMEA and Design for Assembly during virtual prototyping.
- Iterated on component geometry and active-suspension control parameters, and reported the results in technical reports.

## Result

| Result | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Vehicle stability improved by 15% | Simulation |

## Limits

The 15% is a modelled result from the simulation, not a measured vehicle result.
