## The problem

A structural housing for a high-vibration environment, moulded in PA66-GF30. The first concept needed a multi-slide tool, which adds cost and risk.

This is an independent project on my own geometry.

## What I did

I designed the housing in SolidWorks with a uniform 2.5 mm nominal wall, 1.5° to 2.0° draft angles and a 0.6× rib-to-wall ratio. SolidWorks Plastics DFM diagnostics showed three undercut and thin-wall flow restrictions, and I redesigned the internal features to remove them.

## Results

| Result | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Three undercut and thin-wall restrictions resolved | Design result |
| Multi-slide concept converted to a straight-pull 2-plate mould | Design result |
| Hard-tooling cost about 20% lower | Estimate |
