## The problem

JCB builds off-highway machinery. I worked on hydraulic and drivetrain subsystems, where components from several teams have to fit together without clashing, and where every drawing goes out to suppliers.

## What I did

**Packaging and Digital Mock-Up.** I ran packaging studies and used Digital Mock-Up reviews to find spatial and kinematic clashes between hydraulic and drivetrain components, then put targeted design countermeasures in place before the physical pilot build.

**Drawings.** I produced and reviewed more than 40 manufacturing and assembly drawings for hydraulic components and global valve assemblies in Siemens NX, applying BS 8888 and ASME Y14.5 GD&T.

**Bracket lightweighting.** I ran structural FEA on a cast-iron mounting bracket and removed material from low-stress regions.

**Working across sites.** I coordinated CAD outputs, design changes and interface information with offshore design teams in India, using top-down assembly practice and interface-review checkpoints across mating subassemblies.

## Results

| Result | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Approximately US$15,000 of physical pilot-build rework avoided | Estimate |
| Supplier dimensional errors down 14% | Reported |
| Bracket mass down 11%, at a 2.5 factor of safety under a simulated 5 g shock load | Simulation |
| Design-iteration turnaround time down 15% | Workplace result |
