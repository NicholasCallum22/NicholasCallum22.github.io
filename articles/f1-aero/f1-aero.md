## The problem

A BTech project at VIT: how do 2021 and 2022 generation F1 cars interact aerodynamically, and could a 2022-regulation concept perform better in turbulent air?

## What I did

- Modelled a conceptual car to the 2022 FIA regulations in CATIA and SolidWorks.
- Ran CFD in ANSYS Fluent and OpenFOAM to study flow structures, downforce and drag, refining the design from each set of results.
- Proposed a 1.8° rake angle, adjusted wing angles of attack, and revised sidepods, diffuser and endplates.
