## The problem

An MSc group project at the University of Leeds: design a connected and autonomous transportation pod for 10 people (1000 kg), with space for a wheelchair and luggage.

## What I did

I owned the chassis design, the FEA and analysis work, and the integration of the subsystems.

- Designed a 3000 mm × 2000 mm ladder-frame chassis in CAD, and contributed to the exterior body.
- Ran FEA and frontal impact simulations of the concept at 40 km/h and 70 km/h in ANSYS Explicit Dynamics.
- Integrated the chassis with the powertrain, battery, steering and sensor subsystems.

The group shared the rest, and I contributed to it: selecting a Bosch eAxle and a 39.4 kWh LiFePO4 battery pack, a wireless-charging concept, a steer-by-wire system taking camera, LIDAR and radar inputs, and a materials decision matrix comparing aluminium, stainless steel, POM and carbon fibre.

## Limits

This was a concept study, and the impact work was simulation only.
