## The problem

At Caterpillar I worked at the interface between R&D and casting and machining suppliers for powertrain components. One problem I worked on was high-frequency fatigue failure in powertrain vibration mounts.

## What I did

**Failure investigation.** I reviewed the failure modes and the operating load conditions, and supported the development of a reinforced isolation design.

**Model correlation.** I built a non-linear ANSYS Mechanical model of the mount: a hyperelastic Mooney-Rivlin elastomer with frictional contact to the steel bracket, under preload and torque-reaction loads. I then compared it with physical hydraulic test-rig displacement data from reinforced prototype mounts.

The first model under-predicted peak vertical deflection by about 19%. Refining the support-stiffness boundary condition brought the simulated peak deflection within 6% of the measured value.

## Results

| Result | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Reinforced isolation design reported to extend component life by 25% | Reported |
| Simulated peak deflection within 6% of rig measurement, from about 19% under-prediction | Test data |

## Limits

The rig data belongs to the employer, so I keep the measured values for interviews rather than publishing them. The correlation covers peak vertical deflection on the reinforced prototype mounts.
