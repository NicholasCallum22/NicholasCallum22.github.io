## The teams

At VIT I worked on two Shell Eco-marathon student teams at the same time: Team Celerity Solar EV (Oct 2019 to Feb 2022) and Team Eco Titans (Aug 2020 to May 2021).

## Team Celerity Solar EV, design engineer

- Led the aerodynamic bodywork design in CATIA, SolidWorks and ANSYS CFD and FEA, with a reported 15% reduction in drag.
- Optimised the chassis with CAD and ANSYS FEA for a reported 10.5% to 12% weight reduction.
- Managed the bill of materials and led testing and data analysis, contributing to the team ranking 4th in the Indian region and within the top 50 overall in the 2020–21 virtual season, UrbanConcept category.
- Applied DFM and process planning with the manufacturing team, for a reported 20% improvement over the previous production process.
- Modelled the powertrain in MATLAB and Simulink to maximise range, and processed telemetry to correlate multi-body models with accelerometer data.

## Team Eco Titans, mechanical design engineer

- Designed lightweight powertrain-mounting brackets in Fusion 360 and ANSYS for the team's gasoline Prototype vehicle, with a simulated 24% mass reduction at a minimum 2.0 factor of safety under peak cornering and braking loads.
- Contributed to SolidWorks packaging, ANSYS structural validation and safety documentation for the 2021 Virtual Technical Inspection, where first-pass approval was reported for the chassis, steering and powertrain-mounting subsystems.

## Limits

These are student-team results. Several are reported by the team rather than measured independently, and the Eco Titans work was a group effort.
