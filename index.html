SHEET 1 / 4TOOLS Creo Parametric, ExcelSTD ASME Y14.5

1D tolerance stack-up for a hydraulic-drivetrain interface

  • Found a calculated 0.38 mm axial binding risk in a 7-part interface using a closed-loop worst-case and 3σ RSS stack-up.
  • Revised the datum reference frame and moved non-critical shoulders to statistical allocation, securing a calculated 0.15 mm minimum clearance at 85 °C and relaxing those tolerances by 30% (assuming Cpk ≥ 1.33) to cut projected machining cost.
Method, equations and assumptions

Twc = Σ |ti|    Trss = √(Σ ti2)    Cpk = min[(USL − μ)/3σ, (μ − LSL)/3σ]

LOOP
Closed 1D vector loop through 7 parts, with thermal growth at 85 °C.
DECISION
Worst-case kept where binding would be critical; RSS on non-critical shoulders.
ASSUMES
RSS treats dimensions as normal, centred and independent; ±3σ covers 99.73%. Cpk 1.33 is an assumed capability, not measured.
FILES
Creo assembly, part and drawing files; Excel worst-case and RSS workbook.
SHEET 2 / 4TOOLS SolidWorks, SolidWorks PlasticsMATL PA66-GF30

Injection-moulded structural housing

  • Designed a glass-filled nylon housing for high-vibration service with a uniform 2.5 mm wall, 1.5°–2.0° draft and a 0.6× rib-to-wall ratio.
  • Resolved 3 undercut and thin-wall flow restrictions with SolidWorks Plastics, turning a multi-slide tool into a straight-pull 2-plate mould for an estimated ~20% saving in hard-tooling cost.
Method, rules and assumptions

trib = 0.5–0.6 × twall    draft + ≈1° per 0.025 mm texture    PA66-GF30 shrink ≈ 0.3–0.7%

WALL
Uniform 2.5 mm nominal to avoid sinks and differential cooling.
TOOLING
Internal features reworked so no side-actions are needed: a straight-pull 2-plate mould.
ESTIMATE
The ~20% tooling saving is a calculation, not a supplier quote. Shrinkage depends on grade and fibre orientation.
FILES
SolidWorks part and SolidWorks Plastics study.

SHEET 4 / 4TOOLS OpenFOAMCASE H₂–air, 1 atm, 298 K

Reacting-flow CFD validation: hydrogen-air detonation

  • Validated a transient OpenFOAM reacting-flow model (1D/2D shock tube, 9-species Ó Conaire mechanism, 0.1 mm resolution) against NASA CEA and Caltech Chapman-Jouguet data.
  • Matched CJ detonation velocity within 2.85% and CJ pressure within 4.43%.
Set-up and validation basis
DOMAIN
1D/2D planar shock tube, 1.5 m × 0.03 m.
MESH
0.1 mm cells across the induction zone, about 15–20 per half-reaction length; Courant number ≤ 0.2.
CHEMISTRY
Stoichiometric H₂–air at 1 atm and 298 K; Ó Conaire 9-species, 19-reaction mechanism; stiff ODE integration.
REFERENCE
NASA CEA Chapman-Jouguet values and the Caltech detonation database.
LIMIT
CJ pressure is the weakest match (4.43%), so it sets the "within 4.5%" bound.

Next step, proposed as research: extending the validated model to a 3D annular rotating detonation engine combustor.
